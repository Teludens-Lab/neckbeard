The intervention

The learning need

Plenum is a role-governed simulation platform. New participants must find their Session, gain admission, and enter the intended Seat and role. These are separate steps: finding a Session does not mean someone is ready to begin.

Scope and ownership

The project owner selected Plenum and authorized the work. Using Neckbeard, Codex inspected the implementation and drafted a participant job aid, a transfer exercise, and an observation protocol.

From design to evidence

The design decision

A linear “enter code, choose role, begin” outline became a decision-based guide. Participants learn when to accept an invitation, wait for admission, request assignment, or choose an offered role—and how to verify their workspace.

What the work demonstrates

Task analysis, instruction tied to interface states, and assessment of independent performance. The guide traces to a pinned source revision. It is a source-based prototype; live Plenum validation and learner outcomes remain unmeasured.
