The intervention

The learning need

Blue Scribes record Strategic Orientation; Facilitators submit it to White Cell. Learners must distinguish their own choice from a forecast of Red—and a saved handoff from a completed review.

Scope and ownership

The owner selected the Fractured Order deployment. Using Neckbeard, Codex traced its Blue Scribe workflow and produced a job aid, listening edition, narrated explainer, and transfer exercise.

From design to evidence

The design decision

Teach one complete task through three inputs: Blue’s orientation, the forecast of Red’s orientation, and Red’s expected action. End with the saved record and confirmation from the receiving Facilitator.

What the work demonstrates

Role-specific task analysis, a clear handoff boundary, and consistent learning materials across formats. The source is pinned to a clean local checkout. Hosted behavior and learner outcomes are not established.
